# ChronoQuest Player Guide: Getting Started

Welcome to **ChronoQuest**, an interactive World History Timeline Placement Challenge and Era Atlas. In ChronoQuest, you build a living chronological timeline of human civilization by reasoning about how discoveries, empires, and revolutions connect across time.

---

## Core Game Modes & Navigation

ChronoQuest organizes your historical journey into three primary workspaces accessible from the top navigation bar:

1. **Timeline Challenge**: Draw unplaced milestone cards and position them into the correct chronological slot on your expanding timeline board.
2. **Era Atlas**: Explore all 6 canonical eras of world history and study curated events, regions, BCE/CE dates, and historical significance.
3. **Hall of Records**: Compare top completed runs, high scores, and longest placement streaks across difficulty tiers.

---

## How to Play the Timeline Challenge

### Step 1: Configure Your Session
1. Enter your **Player Name** in the challenge setup panel.
2. Select a **Difficulty Tier**:
   - **Explorer**: 5 cards to place, 3 lives, 100 base points per card (+25 per consecutive streak).
   - **Historian**: 8 cards to place, 3 lives, 150 base points per card (+50 per consecutive streak).
   - **Chronomaster**: 12 cards to place, 2 lives, 200 base points per card (+75 per consecutive streak).
3. Optionally choose an **Era Focus** (or keep **All Eras** for a full cross-civilization challenge).
4. Click **Start Timeline Challenge**.

### Step 2: Examine Your Anchor Card and Drawn Card
- Your timeline board begins with **1 face-up Anchor Card** already placed on the timeline, displaying its exact **Year (`BCE` or `CE`)**, **Era**, **Region**, and **Historical Significance**.
- Above the board, your **Drawn Card** shows the event's **Title**, **Historical Era**, **Region**, and a **Clue Summary**—while keeping its exact year hidden until you make your move.

### Step 3: Choose a Chronological Slot
- Inspect the insertion slots (`Place Here`) located **before**, **between**, and **after** the cards currently on your timeline.
- Click the slot where the Drawn Card belongs chronologically:
  - **Correct Placement**: You earn base points plus your active streak bonus, your streak counter increases by 1, and the card locks into place—revealing its exact year and historical significance.
  - **Misplaced Event**: You lose 1 life and your current streak resets to 0. To help you learn as you play, ChronoQuest automatically moves the card into its **true chronological position** on the timeline and reveals its year and significance.

### Step 4: Complete the Chronicle
- **Victory (`Completed`)**: Place every card in your session deck before running out of lives to record a completed chronicle in the **Hall of Records**.
- **Out of Lives (`Game Over`)**: If your remaining lives reach 0, the session concludes and records your earned score and best streak on the leaderboard.

## Studying with the Era Atlas

Select **Era Atlas** at any time to browse the 6 eras of world history:

- **Ancient World** (`3500 BCE – 500 BCE`)
- **Classical Era** (`500 BCE – 500 CE`)
- **Medieval Era** (`500 CE – 1450 CE`)
- **Early Modern Era** (`1450 CE – 1750 CE`)
- **Industrial Age** (`1750 CE – 1914 CE`)
- **Modern Era** (`1914 CE – Present`)

Use the era filter pills to focus on a single historical period and review each event's date, geographic region, summary, and long-term historical impact.
