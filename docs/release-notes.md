# Release Notes

## v0.1.0 — Initial Public Release (2026-11-15)

### Highlights

**ChronoQuest v0.1.0** introduces the interactive **World History Timeline Placement Challenge**, the **6-Era World History Atlas**, and the **Hall of Records** leaderboard.

### What's New

- **Interactive Timeline Placement Challenge**:
  - Start custom gameplay sessions across three difficulty tiers (**Explorer**, **Historian**, and **Chronomaster**) covering either all historical periods or a focused single era.
  - Begin each run with a face-up **Anchor Card** on the timeline and place drawn historical events into chronological slots before, between, or after placed cards.
  - Earn escalating streak bonuses for consecutive accurate placements, with immediate educational feedback revealing each event's `BCE`/`CE` year and historical significance after every turn.
  - Automatic true-slot correction on misplaced cards so players immediately see where an event belongs chronologically.
- **World History Era Atlas**:
  - Browse 24 curated milestones spanning six canonical periods: **Ancient World**, **Classical Era**, **Medieval Era**, **Early Modern Era**, **Industrial Age**, and **Modern Era**.
  - Filter catalog cards by era to study regional context, summaries, and historical significance.
- **Hall of Records Leaderboard**:
  - View top scores, maximum streaks, and completed runs across difficulty tiers, complete with zero-data onboarding guidance for newly initialized environments.
